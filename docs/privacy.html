Privacy Policy for XHabit

Last Updated: 2026-11-26 • App ID: app.xhabit.tracker • github.com/ikianixi/xhabit

XHabit is a local-first, open-source habit tracker. We do not operate any backend servers, do not sell user data, and include zero third-party ads or analytics SDKs.

1. Local Device Storage

Habits, categories, checklists, day modes, To Do List tasks, completion history, and optional journal media (text, images, voice notes) are stored strictly on your device in local SQLite storage and encrypted preferences.

2. Optional Google Account, Drive & Calendar Sync

When you choose to sign in with Google in Settings, XHabit communicates directly between your device and Google APIs using OAuth 2.0. No intermediary XHabit server ever touches your credentials or data.

  • email & profile: Displays your connected Google email inside Settings.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata: Reads and updates only XHabit's hidden app-private folder (xhabit_cloud_sync.json). XHabit cannot access your personal Google Drive files.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events: Optionally creates, updates, and deletes only XHabit habit schedule events in your primary Google Calendar.

XHabit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Optional Obsidian Vault & Update Checks

  • Obsidian Sync: Writes Markdown files strictly inside a dedicated XHabit/ subfolder of the directory you select. Erasing data in XHabit removes only the XHabit/ subfolder.
  • Check for Updates: Queries GitHub's public Releases API only when you tap Check for Updates in Settings.

4. Deletion & Contact

You may disconnect Google, delete XHabit's cloud backup copy, or erase all local data directly in Settings, or revoke permissions at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Questions: GitHub Issues.
